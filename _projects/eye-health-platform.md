This project built data-processing and modeling components for an intelligent eye-health management platform. The work supported large-scale ocular-health data analysis, early diagnosis, and decision support.

My contributions included algorithm design for medical data processing, analytical frameworks for ocular-health datasets, and predictive modeling for autonomous monitoring and management.
