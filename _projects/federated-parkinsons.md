This project developed federated transfer learning methods for early Parkinson's disease warning. The goal was to integrate multi-source health data across institutions while preserving data locality and privacy.

The research addressed personalization, domain heterogeneity, sparse samples, and cross-institutional transfer in healthcare prediction scenarios.
