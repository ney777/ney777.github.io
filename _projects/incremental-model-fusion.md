This project studies model fusion methods for multi-center medical collaboration, with a focus on secure and efficient cooperation across institutions that cannot directly share sensitive clinical data.

My work focuses on intelligent model selection, incremental fusion, multitask collaboration, and privacy-preserving mechanisms for heterogeneous medical imaging and genomics settings.
